Normal — Testing Guide

For the staging release. No technical experience needed — every test says what to do, what should happen, and what a problem looks like.

Before you start
  • Test on staging, not the live site.
  • Press Ctrl+Shift+R (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac) once to load a fresh copy.
  • Staging uses the real database and real money. Use tiny amounts — a few cents. Every transaction is real.
First — which wallet are you testing with?

This decides which tests apply to you. Look at the account drawer (avatar, top right):

  • Normal wallet — the wallet the app created for you, which you unlock with Face ID / fingerprint / a passkey. Do every test in this guide.
  • Your own Stellar wallet — you connected Freighter, Lobstr, Ledger or WalletConnect. Do Parts 1, 2, 3 and Part 6. Skip anything marked Normal wallet only.

Connected wallets work with XLM and USDC only — that is by design, not a bug. Bitcoin, Ethereum and Solana need the Normal wallet, because only it can sign on those networks. If you have both, treat yourself as a Normal-wallet tester and do everything.

What changed, in one paragraph

We made the app fetch data more efficiently and fixed several bugs where numbers appeared late or briefly showed wrong values. Almost nothing should look different. That is the point: the main way we find a problem is if something that used to work now looks wrong. Trust your instincts — if it looks odd, report it.

Two things are genuinely new and need real testing rather than a glance: swapping from Bitcoin works again (test 4.8), and connected wallets such as Freighter and Lobstr now record their transactions properly (Part 6).

☐6.2Deposit a small amount into Savings, then look at ActivityThe deposit appears, and your savings balance is correct

Report immediately if:
  • a swap or savings deposit never appears in Activity, even after reloading (6.1, 6.2) — that is the exact bug we just fixed;
  • picking BTC, ETH or SOL lets you start a swap, or asks you to create a new wallet (6.3) — it should simply be greyed out;
  • your wallet is missing from Settings → Linked accounts (6.4);
  • your Stellar address changes on its own — a different address, or different XLM/USDC amounts, after reloading or reconnecting. Screenshot both addresses if you can.
Not bugs — please do not report these:
  • No Bitcoin, Ethereum or Solana in your portfolio. Connected wallets hold XLM and USDC only.
  • Not being able to send BTC/ETH/SOL, or receive Bitcoin.
  • Cross-chain swaps being unavailable — that is test 6.3 working correctly.
  • Lobstr or WalletConnect asking you to reconnect after a page reload.

How to report a problem

Send one message with as many of these as you can:

  1. The test number (for example “3.2”)
  2. What you saw, and what the guide said should happen
  3. Which asset (BTC, XLM…) and roughly what time
  4. A screenshot of the whole browser window
  5. Console screenshot — see below. This is often the single most useful thing.
  6. For anything in Part 4 or Part 6, the transaction ID if you have one
  7. Which wallet you were using — the Normal wallet, or a connected one (say which: Freighter, Lobstr, Ledger, WalletConnect)

How to take a console screenshot (30 seconds, no experience needed)

  1. Press F12 on your keyboard (on Mac: Cmd+Option+I).
  2. A panel opens. Click the tab labelled Console.
  3. Screenshot anything shown in red.
  4. Press F12 again to close it.

If there is nothing red, that is useful too — say so.

Do not worry about whether it is “really” a bug. If it looked wrong to you, report it. A false alarm costs a minute; a missed bug costs far more. You are not expected to know which is which.

If you only have 15 minutes

With the Normal wallet — do these, in this order. They cover the highest-risk changes:

  1. 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 — the three screens must agree
  2. 3.1 — deposit to savings, watch all three numbers
  3. 4.1 — send a small amount of XLM
  4. 4.8 — swap from Bitcoin, and count the passkey prompts

With your own connected wallet — do these instead:

  1. 1.1, 1.2, 1.3 — the three screens must agree
  2. 6.1 — swap XLM ↔ USDC and check it lands in Activity
  3. 6.3 — confirm BTC/ETH/SOL is greyed out rather than letting you continue
