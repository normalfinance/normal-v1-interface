4 · Thresholds — measured, not guessed

  • Base: 65 relayer transactions have cost 0.000052 ETH in total (~$0.12) — about 0.0000008 ETH each. The 0.002 ETH threshold is still thousands of mints of headroom.
  • Stellar: Soroban fees run 0.05–0.5 XLM per mint and 1 XLM is locked as the account reserve, so 20 XLM ≈ 40 mints in the worst case. Suggested top-up: 50 XLM.
  • Ethereum: excluded from alerting — the CCTP pivot is hardcoded to Base (use-cctp-engine.tsx:857) and the account has never been funded or used. If an Ethereum destination is ever enabled, this row turns into a real threshold.
