Watching The Wrong Wallet

Branch feat/swap-matrix-hardening, run 11 — found it. Your screenshot had the answer in it.

1 · The answer to “what's the point of the step?”

Fair question, and the honest answer is that the step was doing its job perfectly — it was just watching a wallet that the swap never touches. So it waited, saw nothing move, waited some more, ran out of patience and ticked itself green. Every time. No number of fixes to the caching underneath could ever have helped, which is why my last three attempts kept missing.

2 · What your screenshot showed

[ Normal wallet · 28.70 ]  [ Lobstr · 0.00 ]
                 28.706530 USDC · Normal wallet

You are signed in with an external wallet (Lobstr), and you chose to swap from your Normal wallet — the companion. That distinction is the whole bug.

The app keeps those two wallets' balances in two separate places, deliberately:

fieldholds
assetsthe connected wallet — Lobstr, 0.00
companionStellar.assetsthe Normal wallet — the 28.70 that actually swapped

The gate compared only assets. So it sat there asking “has the Lobstr balance changed?” about a swap that moved the Normal wallet:

before:  USDC 0.00, XLM 0.00      (Lobstr)
after:   USDC 0.00, XLM 0.00      (Lobstr — of course it didn't change)
verdict: not fresh yet → wait → ask again → wait → give up → tick Done
Permanently false, not intermittently
This was not a race or a timing window. For an external-wallet user swapping from their Normal wallet, the condition the gate waits on can never become true. It always burned its full budget and always gave up — and then Done ticked, with the balance updating whenever the ordinary background refresh next came round. That is the 5–10 seconds you kept seeing.

3 · The fix

The gate now watches both wallets and both sides of the swap — four numbers:

USDC:slot=0,normal=28.706530 | XLM:slot=0,normal=5.1
                    ↓ after the swap
USDC:slot=0,normal=27.701530 | XLM:slot=0,normal=10.397652   ← changed

Only one of the four moves on any given swap, so including all of them means the gate never has to work out in advance which wallet is paying. It just notices that something moved.

That fingerprint is now a tested function rather than a line buried in the card, and the tests include your exact case — Lobstr flat at 0.00 while the companion swaps — so this particular blindness cannot come back. One test also checks the two wallets are kept separate rather than added together, since summing them would let a rise in one hide a fall in the other.

The mistake I kept making
Each round I confirmed the gate's plumbing was sound and went looking one layer deeper. What I never checked until your screenshot was the most basic thing of all: whether the number the gate watches is the number on your screen. It wasn't. The screenshot showed two wallet chips and I should have asked that question in round one.
